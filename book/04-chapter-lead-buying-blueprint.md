# Chapter 4: The Lead Buying Blueprint

## What a "Lead" Actually Is and Why You're Buying Them

A lead is simply the contact information of a business owner or decision-maker who needs a website. That's it. A name, a phone number, an email, and ideally the name and type of their business.

Why buy leads instead of finding them yourself? The same reason a restaurant buys ingredients instead of growing them. **Time is money.** You could spend 3 hours a day scraping Google Maps for local businesses, or you could spend $50 on Fiverr and get 100 pre-qualified leads delivered to your inbox in 24 hours.

When you buy leads, you're buying time. And in this business, your time is worth $500-$2,000 per hour once you're closing deals. So paying $50 for 100 leads isn't an expense — it's the smartest investment you'll ever make.

## The 5 Types of Fiverr Lead Sellers (And Which Ones Are Gold)

Not all lead sellers on Fiverr are created equal. Here's a breakdown of what you'll find:

### Type 1: The Google Maps Scraper ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**What they do:** They use software to scrape business listings from Google Maps based on your criteria — location, industry, and whether or not the business has a website.

**Why they're great:** You can target specific industries (restaurants, dentists, salons) in specific cities. The data is fresh because it comes directly from Google.

**What you get:** Business name, owner name (sometimes), phone number, email (sometimes), address, website (if they have one), Google rating.

**Cost:** $25-$75 for 200-500 leads.

**How to find them:** Search Fiverr for "Google Maps leads," "local business leads," or "B2B lead generation."

**Pro tip:** Ask for businesses that do NOT have a website, or businesses whose websites are not mobile-friendly. These are your warmest prospects.

### Type 2: The LinkedIn Scraper ⭐⭐⭐

**What they do:** They use tools to extract business owner contact information from LinkedIn.

**Why they're decent:** You get direct decision-maker contacts, not just general business info.

**What you get:** Name, title, company, email, LinkedIn profile URL.

**Cost:** $30-$100 for 100-300 leads.

**How to find them:** Search Fiverr for "LinkedIn leads" or "LinkedIn email extraction."

**Limitation:** These leads tend to be more corporate. They work better for selling to established businesses, not mom-and-pop shops.

### Type 3: The Web Scraper ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**What they do:** They build custom scrapes of business directories like Yelp, Yellow Pages, BBB, or industry-specific directories. The BEST ones specifically find businesses with outdated or non-existent websites.

**Why they're gold:** These are the most targeted leads you can buy. Some sellers will literally check each business's website and flag ones that are ugly, broken, or missing.

**What you get:** Business name, contact info, current website URL (for analysis), industry, location.

**Cost:** $50-$150 for 100-300 targeted leads.

**How to find them:** Search Fiverr for "business leads no website," "web design leads," or "businesses needing websites."

**This is your #1 source.** These sellers are doing the prospecting work for you and delivering people who literally need what you're selling.

### Type 4: The Email List Seller ⭐⭐

**What they do:** They sell pre-compiled email lists of businesses by category.

**Why they're mediocre:** The lists are often old, shared with many buyers, and the emails may have already been blasted by other sellers.

**What you get:** Name, email, sometimes phone number.

**Cost:** $10-$30 for 500-1,000 emails.

**Avoid unless:** You're on a very tight budget and want to practice your email outreach without worrying about "wasting" good leads.

### Type 5: The Appointment Setter ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**What they do:** They don't just find leads — they contact the businesses, pitch your services, and book actual phone appointments for you.

**Why they're incredible:** You skip the hardest part (cold outreach) and jump straight to warm conversations with interested business owners.

**What you get:** Pre-scheduled phone calls or Zoom meetings with qualified prospects.

**Cost:** $100-$300 for 5-15 booked appointments.

**How to find them:** Search Fiverr for "appointment setting," "cold calling service," or "lead generation calls."

**Best for:** When you're making enough money to reinvest and want to scale fast. If someone else is booking your calls, your only job is showing up and closing.

## How to Find, Vet, and Negotiate with Lead Providers

Here's your step-by-step process for buying leads on Fiverr:

### Step 1: Search with the Right Keywords

Use these Fiverr search terms:
- "small business leads"
- "business leads no website"
- "Google Maps scraping"
- "local business contact list"
- "b2b leads [your target industry]"
- "web design leads"
- "appointment setting b2b"

### Step 2: Filter and Sort

- Sort by "Best Selling" or "Recommended"
- Filter by seller level: Level 2 or Top Rated Seller
- Look for sellers with 100+ reviews and 4.8+ star rating
- Check that they've delivered recently (not a dead gig)

### Step 3: Read Reviews Carefully

Don't just look at the star rating. Read the actual reviews. Look for:
- "Leads were accurate" — this tells you the data is real
- "Replied quickly" — good communication
- "Great quality" — the leads actually convert
- "Got responses from these leads" — other buyers are having success

Red flags:
- "Many emails bounced" — stale data
- "Duplicate leads" — lazy scraping
- "Took forever to deliver" — unreliable
- Reviews that all sound the same — could be fake

### Step 4: Message Before You Buy

Always send a message before placing an order. Here's a template:

> "Hi! I'm looking for leads of small businesses that either don't have a website or have an outdated one. I'm targeting [city/region] and interested in [industries like restaurants, salons, dentists, contractors]. Can you provide leads with phone numbers and emails? How fresh is the data? What's your turnaround time?"

A good seller will respond within hours with helpful, specific answers. If they give vague responses or take days to reply, move on.

### Step 5: Start Small

Never buy the biggest package first. Start with the smallest order (usually 50-100 leads for $25-$50). Test the quality. If the leads are good:
- Contact them and track your results
- Measure how many have valid phone numbers and emails
- See how many actually need a website

If the quality checks out, scale up. If not, try a different seller.

### Step 6: Negotiate for Volume

Once you find a good seller, ask for a custom deal:

> "Hey, I really liked the quality of my last order. I'd like to buy leads on an ongoing basis — 200 leads per week. Can you give me a discount for regular orders?"

Most sellers will knock 20-30% off for repeat business. Some will even create a custom package just for you.

Let me break down the ROI of buying leads:

| Stage | Count | Rate |

| Leads purchased | 150 | — |
| Valid contacts | 120 | 80% (some will have wrong numbers) |
| Responses/Conversations | 24 | 20% of valid contacts |
| Interested prospects | 12 | 50% of conversations |
| Deals closed | 4 | 33% of interested prospects |
| Revenue ($2,500 avg) | $10,000 | — |
| Lead cost | -$75 | — |
| Developer costs (4 × $200) | -$800 | — |
| **Net profit** | **$9,125** | — |

You spent $75 on leads and made $9,125 in profit. That's a **121x return on investment.**

Even if you're terrible at sales and only close ONE deal from those 150 leads, you still make $2,500 minus $75 for leads minus $200 for the developer = **$2,225 profit from a $75 investment.**

There is no stock, no crypto, no real estate deal that consistently gives you those kinds of returns. And you can do this every single month.

## Your First Lead Order: A Walkthrough

Let me walk you through your very first lead purchase:

1. **Go to Fiverr.com** and create an account if you don't have one

2. **Search for:** "small business leads no website"

3. **Pick a seller** with Level 2 or Top Rated status and 4.7+ stars

4. **Message them:** "Hi! I'm looking for 50-100 leads of small businesses in [your city or state] that don't have a website or have a very outdated one. I'm interested in restaurants, salons, contractors, and dentists. Can you provide business name, owner name if available, phone number, and email? How quickly can you deliver?"

5. **Place the order** for the smallest package (usually $25-$50)

6. **When leads arrive:** Download the file (usually CSV or Excel format) and immediately import it into your Lead Tracker spreadsheet (Chapter 5)

7. **Start outreach** the same day or next morning

Don't overthink this. Your first batch of leads won't be perfect. Some numbers will be wrong. Some emails will bounce. That's normal. The point is to get leads flowing into your system so you can start having conversations and closing deals.

The clock starts when you buy your first batch of leads. Let's make it today.

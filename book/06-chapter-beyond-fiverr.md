# Chapter 6: Beyond Fiverr — Stacking Lead Sources

## Why You Need More Than One Lead Source

Fiverr is your starting point, not your only point. Relying on a single lead source is like having a car with only one tire. It might roll, but it won't take you far.

The most successful website sellers I know have 3-5 lead sources working simultaneously. Some are paid. Some are free. Some are active (you do the work). Some are passive (leads come to you). The combination is what creates a steady, unstoppable flow of prospects into your Excel pipeline.

Here are the four additional lead sources that work best alongside Fiverr lead buying.

## Facebook Groups: The Free Lead Machine

This is the most underrated lead source in the website selling business. And it's completely free.

### How It Works

There are thousands of Facebook Groups where small business owners hang out. Groups for local entrepreneurs, groups for specific industries, groups for people starting businesses, and groups for people explicitly looking for website help.

In these groups, business owners regularly post things like:
- "Does anyone know a good website designer?"
- "I need a website for my bakery, any recommendations?"
- "How much should a website cost?"
- "My website looks terrible, what should I do?"

Every single one of those posts is a lead. And unlike Fiverr leads, these people are actively asking for help.

### Step-by-Step Setup

**1. Join 15-25 Facebook Groups**

Search Facebook for:
- "[Your City] Small Business Owners"
- "[Your City] Entrepreneurs"
- "Small Business Website Help"
- "Entrepreneur Support Group"
- "[Industry] Business Owners" (e.g., "Restaurant Owners Group")
- "I Need a Website"
- "Web Design Clients"
- "Small Business Marketing"

Join as many as possible. Most approve you within 24 hours.

**2. Set Up Notifications**

For each group, click the notification bell and select "All Posts" or "Highlights." This way you'll get notified when someone posts about needing a website.

**3. Engage Before You Sell**

Do NOT join a group and immediately start posting "I build websites, hire me!" You'll get banned and ignored.

Instead, spend the first week:
- Commenting on other people's posts with helpful advice
- Answering questions about online presence, marketing, etc.
- Being a genuinely helpful member of the community

**4. Respond to Lead Posts**

When someone posts asking for website help, respond with something like:

> "Hey [Name]! I help small businesses get professional websites up and running. I've done a bunch of sites for [their industry] — happy to show you some examples and chat about what you're looking for. Mind if I send you a DM?"

Keep it casual. Keep it helpful. Don't be salesy.

**5. Take It to DMs**

Once they say yes, move to direct messages. Ask about their business, what they need, and what their budget looks like. Add them to your Excel spreadsheet as a lead.

### Expected Results

Spend 15-20 minutes per day on Facebook Groups and you can realistically generate 5-15 new leads per week. These leads are often warmer than Fiverr leads because they're actively looking for help.

Cost: $0

## Google Maps + Cold Email: Local Businesses Begging for Websites

This is the DIY version of what Fiverr lead sellers do for you. It takes more time, but it's free and incredibly effective.

### How It Works

1. Open Google Maps
2. Search for a type of business in your area (e.g., "plumber Dallas TX")
3. Look at the results
4. Click through to each business's website (or note that they don't have one)
5. If they don't have a website, or their website looks like it was built in 2005, add them to your lead list

### The Process

**Step 1: Pick an Industry**

Start with industries that desperately need websites but often don't have good ones:
- Plumbers and HVAC contractors
- Landscaping companies
- Auto repair shops
- Restaurants and food trucks
- Hair salons and barbershops
- Dentists and chiropractors
- Dog groomers and pet services
- Cleaning companies
- Tutors and music teachers
- Local retailers

**Step 2: Search Google Maps**

Type "[industry] [city]" into Google Maps. You'll get 20-100+ results.

**Step 3: Evaluate Each Business**

For each result, check:
- Do they have a website listed? If no → hot lead
- If yes, click the website. Is it mobile-friendly? Does it look modern? Does it load fast?
- If the website is terrible → warm lead
- Note their phone number, address, and any email you can find

**Step 4: Find Their Email**

For businesses without an obvious email:
- Check their Google listing for contact info
- Check their Facebook page (most businesses have one)
- Use their contact form if they have a crappy website
- Search "[business name] [city] email" on Google

**Step 5: Send a Cold Email**

Here's a template that works:

> Subject: Quick question about [Business Name]'s online presence
>
> Hi [Name/Owner],
>
> I was searching for [industry] businesses in [city] and came across [Business Name]. I noticed you don't currently have a website [OR "your current website could use an update"].
>
> I help local businesses like yours get professional websites that bring in new customers. I recently built a site for a [similar business type] in the area and they saw a 40% increase in online inquiries within the first month.
>
> Would you be open to a quick 5-minute chat about what a modern website could do for your business? No pressure at all — just a friendly conversation.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]
> [Your Phone Number]

**Step 6: Follow Up**

If no response in 3 days, send a follow-up:

> Subject: Re: Quick question about [Business Name]'s online presence
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Just bumping this to the top of your inbox. I know you're busy — just wanted to see if getting a professional website for [Business Name] is something you'd be interested in exploring.
>
> Happy to show you some examples of what I've done for other [industry] businesses. Takes 5 minutes.
>
> [Your Name]

### Expected Results

Spend 1 hour searching Google Maps and you can find 30-50 leads. Send emails, and expect a 10-15% response rate. From 50 emails, you'll get 5-7 responses, and 2-3 will become real conversations.

Cost: $0

## Partnerships with Designers, Marketers, and Print Shops

This is the most overlooked lead source, and potentially the most powerful one for long-term, hands-free lead generation.

### The Concept

There are thousands of professionals who regularly work with small business owners but DON'T offer website services:

- **Graphic designers** who make logos, business cards, and flyers
- **Social media managers** who handle Instagram and Facebook
- **Print shops** that make signs, banners, and marketing materials
- **Photographers** who do headshots and product photos
- **Bookkeepers and accountants** who work with small businesses
- **Insurance agents** who sell to local business owners
- **Commercial real estate agents** who work with new businesses

All of these people have clients who need websites. And they often get asked, "Do you know anyone who can build me a website?"

### How to Set Up Partnerships

**Step 1: Make a List**

Search Google or Yelp for graphic designers, print shops, and marketing consultants in your area. Make a list of 20-30.

**Step 2: Reach Out**

> "Hi [Name], I help small businesses get professional websites. I know you work with a lot of local businesses, and I bet some of them ask about websites. I'd love to set up a referral arrangement — for every client you send my way who signs up, I'll pay you a $200-$500 referral fee. Would you be interested in chatting about it?"

**Step 3: Make It Easy**

Give them:
- Your business card
- A one-page flyer they can hand to clients
- A simple way to refer (email intro, phone number, or a shared form)

**Step 4: Pay Promptly**

When a referral turns into a deal, pay the referral fee IMMEDIATELY. This builds trust and motivates them to keep sending people your way.

### Expected Results

A single good partnership can bring you 1-3 referrals per month. With 5-10 active partners, that's 5-30 leads per month — and these are the warmest leads imaginable because they come with a personal recommendation.

Cost: $200-$500 per closed referral (paid from your profit)

## Referral Systems That Bring Leads to YOU

The ultimate lead source is referrals from happy clients. Once you've delivered a few websites and your clients are thrilled, you can turn them into a lead generation army.

### The Referral Ask

After delivering a website and getting positive feedback, say:

> "I'm so glad you love it! Hey, quick question — do you know any other business owners who might need a website? For every person you refer who becomes a client, I'll give you a free month of maintenance [or $100-$200 credit or cash]."

### The Referral Card

Create a simple business card or flyer that your clients can hand out:

> **Need a professional website for your business?**
> [Your Name] helped me get my business online.
> Contact: [Your Phone] | [Your Email]
> Mention [Client Name] for a special discount!

### The Automated Referral Email

30 days after delivering a website, send this email:

> Subject: Quick favor + a thank-you gift for you
>
> Hi [Client Name],
>
> Hope you're loving your new website! I wanted to check in and make sure everything is running smoothly.
>
> Also — I'm growing my business through word of mouth, and I'd love your help. If you know any fellow business owners who need a website, I'd really appreciate a referral. As a thank-you, I'll give you [incentive] for each referral that becomes a client.
>
> You can just reply with their name and phone number/email, and I'll take it from there!
>
> Thanks so much,
> [Your Name]

### Expected Results

A satisfied client will refer 1-3 people over the life of your relationship. With 20 happy clients, you could get 2-5 referrals per month without spending a dime on lead generation.

Cost: $0 (or whatever referral incentive you choose)

## The Lead Source Stack

Here's what your lead generation machine looks like when all sources are running:

| Source | Leads/Month | Cost/Month | Quality |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| Fiverr lead buying | 100-200 | $100-$200 | Medium |
| Facebook Groups | 20-60 | $0 | High |
| Google Maps + cold email | 50-100 | $0 | Medium-High |
| Partnerships | 5-30 | $200-500/referral | Very High |
| Client referrals | 5-20 | $0-$200 | Highest |
| **Total** | **180-410** | **$100-$900** | — |

With 180-410 leads per month flowing into your Excel spreadsheet, you will NEVER run out of people to talk to. The pipeline will be so full that your biggest challenge will be choosing which leads to prioritize.

And that's a beautiful problem to have.

## Adding Services: The Revenue Multiplier

Once you're consistently closing website deals, you're sitting on a goldmine of additional services you can sell to the same clients. Each of these can be outsourced on Fiverr or through white-label agencies.

### Service 1: Logo Design

Many small businesses either don't have a logo or have a terrible one.

- **When to pitch:** During the website consultation, if their logo is bad or non-existent
- **What it costs you:** $25-$75 on Fiverr
- **What you charge:** $200-$500
- **Your profit:** $125-$425

> "Before we start on the website, let's make sure your brand looks professional. I can have our design team create a stunning logo for you. It usually runs about $300 and includes 3 concepts to choose from."

### Service 2: SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

Every business owner wants to show up on Google. Very few understand how.

- **When to pitch:** After the website is delivered and they ask about Google
- **What it costs you:** $100-$300/month (outsourced to Fiverr SEO specialist)
- **What you charge:** $300-$800/month
- **Your profit:** $200-$500/month (RECURRING)

> "Now that your website is live, the next step is making sure people find it on Google. I offer an SEO package starting at $399/month. My team handles everything — keyword research, content optimization, Google My Business management, and monthly reporting."

SEO is one of the highest-margin add-on services because it's recurring AND outsourceable.

### Service 3: Google Ads Management

For businesses that want immediate results (not willing to wait for SEO), Google Ads is the answer.

- **When to pitch:** When a client says "I need more customers NOW"
- **What it costs you:** $100-$200/month management + client pays ad spend directly
- **What you charge:** $300-$500/month management fee
- **Your profit:** $100-$400/month (RECURRING)

> "If you want calls and customers this week, not in 6 months, Google Ads is the way to go. I manage campaigns for businesses like yours — you set the budget for the ads, and my team handles the strategy, setup, and optimization. Management fee is $400/month."

### Service 4: Social Media Management

Business owners know they "should be on social media" but have no idea how to do it consistently.

- **What it costs you:** $100-$250/month (VA or Fiverr specialist)
- **What you charge:** $300-$700/month
- **Your profit:** $200-$450/month (RECURRING)

### Service 5: Content Writing

Blog posts, website copy, email newsletters — all things businesses need and don't have time to write.

- **What it costs you:** $10-$50 per piece (Fiverr writers)
- **What you charge:** $100-$300 per piece or $200-$500/month for ongoing content
- **Your profit:** $90-$250 per piece

## White-Labeling Everything and Becoming an "Agency"

At some point, you'll stop calling yourself a freelancer and start calling yourself an agency. The difference? Perception and pricing.

### The Agency Mindset

When you're a freelancer, clients think:
- "You're just one person"
- "You should be cheap"
- "You'll be overwhelmed easily"

When you're an agency, clients think:
- "You have a team behind you"
- "You can handle any project"
- "You're established and professional"

Same business model. Same spreadsheet. Same Fiverr developers. Completely different perception and pricing power.

### What "Becoming an Agency" Actually Means

1. **Create a business name** that sounds like a team (e.g., "Summit Digital" not "John's Websites")
2. **Build a simple agency website** (use one of your Fiverr developers — eat your own cooking)
3. **Use "we" language** — "We build websites" not "I build websites"
4. **Get a professional email** — hello@summidigital.com instead of john47@gmail.com
5. **White-label your developers** — they work under your agency brand

### White-Label Service Providers

For each service, find a reliable white-label provider:

| Service | White-Label Source | Your Cost | Client Pays |
|---------|-------------------|-----------|-------------|
| Web design | Fiverr developers | $150-$300 | $2,500-$7,000 |
| SEO | Fiverr / white-label SEO agency | $100-$300/mo | $400-$800/mo |
| Google Ads | Fiverr / white-label PPC agency | $100-$200/mo | $300-$500/mo |
| Social media | VA + Canva templates | $100-$250/mo | $300-$700/mo |
| Content writing | Fiverr writers | $20-$50/piece | $150-$300/piece |
| Logo design | Fiverr designers | $25-$75 | $200-$500 |

Everything is outsourced. Everything is under your brand. You are the face of the agency. You close deals and manage client relationships. Your team does everything else.

Their secret: Division of labor. The salesperson sells. The operator operates. If you have a partner, this business can grow even faster.

## Your 12-Month Plan to Financial Freedom

Here's the month-by-month roadmap:

### Months 1-2: Foundation
- Set up spreadsheet, find developers, buy first leads
- Close 1-3 deals per month
- Learn the script, handle objections, build confidence
- **Income: $2,000-$6,000/month**

### Months 3-4: Momentum
- Refine your process, increase lead volume
- Close 3-4 deals per month
- Start pitching monthly maintenance to every client
- 5-10 maintenance clients
- **Income: $5,000-$10,000/month**

### Months 5-6: Scaling
- Hire a part-time VA
- Increase lead sources (add Facebook Groups, Google Maps)
- Close 4-6 deals per month
- 10-15 maintenance clients
- **Income: $8,000-$15,000/month**

### Months 7-9: Agency Mode
- Create your agency brand (name, website, email)
- Start offering additional services (SEO, Google Ads)
- Close 5-7 deals per month
- 15-25 maintenance clients
- **Income: $12,000-$20,000/month**

### Months 10-12: Wealth Building
- Hire a full-time VA or second VA
- Scale maintenance to 25-35 clients
- Recurring revenue alone covers your basic expenses
- Close 6-8 deals per month
- **Income: $15,000-$25,000/month**

**Year 1 Total: $100,000-$200,000**

### Year 2 and Beyond
- 50+ maintenance clients ($7,000+/month recurring)
- Full service agency offering websites, SEO, ads, social media
- Multiple VAs and developers on your team
- You work 10-15 hours per week, mostly on strategy and key client calls
- **Income: $200,000-$400,000/year**

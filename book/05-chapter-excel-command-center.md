## Setting Up Your Lead Tracker

Your Lead Tracker has five main sections, each as a separate tab in the spreadsheet:

### Tab 1: Lead Pipeline

This is your main working tab. Every lead goes here the moment you get their info.

**Columns:**

| Column | What Goes Here | Example |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| A: Lead # | Auto-numbered ID | 001 |
| B: Date Added | When you added this lead | 03/15/2026 |
| C: Business Name | Name of the business | Tony's Pizza |
| D: Contact Name | Owner or decision-maker | Tony Rossi |
| E: Phone | Phone number | (555) 123-4567 |
| F: Email | Email address | tony@tonyspizza.com |
| G: Industry | Type of business | Restaurant |
| H: City/State | Location | Dallas, TX |
| I: Has Website? | Yes/No/Outdated | Outdated |
| J: Current Website | URL if they have one | tonyspizza.biz |
| K: Lead Source | Where the lead came from | Fiverr Batch #3 |
| L: Status | Current pipeline stage | See status guide below |
| M: Last Contact | Date of last outreach | 03/18/2026 |
| N: Next Follow-Up | When to contact next | 03/21/2026 |
| O: Notes | Important details | "Interested but wants to wait until after Easter" |
| P: Deal Value | Proposed or quoted price | $2,500 |

**Status Options for Column L:**

- **NEW** — Just added, haven't contacted yet
- **CONTACTED** — Sent first outreach (email, call, or message)
- **FOLLOW-UP** — Waiting for response or scheduled follow-up
- **INTERESTED** — Responded positively, conversation happening
- **PROPOSAL SENT** — Sent pricing/proposal
- **NEGOTIATING** — Discussing terms
- **CLOSED-WON** — Deal closed, deposit received
- **CLOSED-LOST** — Said no (for now)
- **NOT QUALIFIED** — Wrong number, out of business, etc.

## Color-Coding Your Way to $10K Months

Color coding is what turns a boring spreadsheet into a visual command center. Here's the system:

### Lead Pipeline Colors

In your Lead Pipeline tab, color the entire row based on the Status column:

| Status | Color | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| NEW | White | Untouched — needs first contact |
| CONTACTED | Light Blue | Ball is in their court |
| FOLLOW-UP | Yellow | YOU need to act — follow up today! |
| INTERESTED | Light Green | Warm lead — prioritize! |
| PROPOSAL SENT | Orange | Hot lead — follow up aggressively |
| NEGOTIATING | Dark Orange | Almost there — close this! |
| CLOSED-WON | Bright Green | Money! Move to Active Projects |
| CLOSED-LOST | Light Red | Not now — revisit in 30-60 days |
| NOT QUALIFIED | Gray | Dead lead — ignore |

When you open your spreadsheet every morning, your eyes should be immediately drawn to:
1. **Yellow rows** — these people need a follow-up TODAY
2. **Orange rows** — you sent a proposal, follow up and close
3. **Green rows** — warm leads, keep the conversation going
4. **White rows** — cold leads that need first contact

This visual system means you never have to think about "what should I do today?" You just open the spreadsheet and the colors tell you.

## The Daily Spreadsheet Routine

Here's exactly how you use your spreadsheet every single day:

### Morning Check-In (10 minutes)

1. Open your Lead Pipeline tab
2. Sort by "Next Follow-Up" date
3. Identify all leads with today's date or earlier — these are your priority
4. Count your yellow and orange rows — these are your hottest opportunities

### Outreach Block (30-60 minutes)

1. Start with follow-ups (yellow and orange rows)
2. Then move to new outreach (white rows)
3. After each call/email, update the row:
   - Change the Status
   - Update "Last Contact" date
   - Set a new "Next Follow-Up" date
   - Add notes about the conversation

### End of Day Update (5 minutes)

1. Review any responses you received
2. Update statuses
3. Move any CLOSED-WON deals to the Active Projects tab
4. Glance at your Revenue Dashboard — watch those numbers grow

### Weekly Review (15 minutes, every Sunday)

1. Review your Revenue Dashboard
2. Count: How many leads did you add this week? How many conversations? How many deals?
3. Identify bottlenecks: Not enough leads? Not enough conversations? Not closing enough?
4. Plan next week: How many leads do you need to buy? Who needs follow-up Monday morning?

## Pro Tips for Spreadsheet Power Users

### Tip 1: Use Filters
Set up filter buttons on your Lead Pipeline header row. This lets you instantly filter by Status (show me only INTERESTED leads), by Industry (show me only restaurants), or by Next Follow-Up date.

### Tip 2: Use Conditional Formatting
Instead of manually coloring rows, set up conditional formatting rules. When Status = "FOLLOW-UP," automatically color the row yellow. This saves time and ensures consistency.

### Tip 3: Add a "Days Since Last Contact" Column
Create a formula: `=TODAY() - M2` (where M2 is the "Last Contact" column). If this number is greater than 7, that lead is going cold and needs attention.

### Tip 4: Create a "Hot Leads" View
Add a filter or separate view that only shows leads with Status = INTERESTED, PROPOSAL SENT, or NEGOTIATING. This is your money view — the deals that are closest to closing.

### Tip 5: Back Up Weekly
Copy your spreadsheet to a backup folder every Sunday. Or use Google Sheets, which auto-saves and has version history built in. Losing your spreadsheet = losing your business. Don't let that happen.

### Tip 6: Google Sheets vs. Excel
Both work. Google Sheets has the advantage of being cloud-based (access from anywhere, auto-saves, easy to share). Excel has more powerful features for complex formulas. My recommendation: **start with Google Sheets.** It's free, it's everywhere, and it does everything you need.

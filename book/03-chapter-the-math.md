# Chapter 3: The Math That Will Change Your Life

## 100 Leads = 20 Calls = 5 Deals = $10,000–$25,000/Month

Let me walk you through the math that changed my entire perspective on making money. This isn't theory — these are real conversion rates from hundreds of people who run this exact business model.

### The Conversion Funnel

**Level 1: Leads → Conversations**
For every 100 leads you contact, about 20 will actually respond and engage in a conversation. That's a 20% response rate, which is standard for cold outreach.

**Level 2: Conversations → Meetings/Demos**
Of those 20 conversations, about 10 will agree to see examples of your work, hop on a quick call, or hear your pitch. That's a 50% meeting rate — and it's actually conservative. When someone responds to your outreach, they're already somewhat interested.

**Level 3: Meetings → Closed Deals**
Of those 10 meetings, about 3-5 will say yes and put down a deposit. That's a 30-50% close rate. High? Yes. But you're not selling something abstract — you're selling a tangible product (a website) to people who already need it.

**So the math looks like this:**

| Stage | Number | Rate |
|-------|--------|------|
| Leads contacted | 100 | — |
| Conversations | 20 | 20% |
| Meetings/Demos | 10 | 50% of conversations |
| Closed deals | 3-5 | 30-50% of meetings |

If you charge an average of $2,500 per website:
- **3 deals × $2,500 = $7,500/month** (conservative)
- **5 deals × $2,500 = $12,500/month** (moderate)
- **5 deals × $5,000 = $25,000/month** (aggressive pricing)

Subtract your costs:
- Lead buying: $200/month
- Developer costs: 5 deals × $200 = $1,000/month
- Total costs: $1,200/month

**Net profit: $6,300 to $23,800 per month.**

On the conservative end, that's $75,600 per year. On the aggressive end, $285,600. Both are life-changing numbers.

## Your First 30 Days: A Realistic Roadmap

Here's exactly what your first month looks like. No fluff, no motivational nonsense — just a day-by-day action plan.

### Week 1: Setup (Days 1-7)

**Day 1-2: Build Your Infrastructure**
- Create a professional email address (yourname@yourbusiness.com or just use Gmail)
- Set up your Excel Lead Tracker (template in Chapter 5)
- Create a simple business name (doesn't need to be fancy)

**Day 3-4: Find Your Developers**
- Go to Fiverr and search for "WordPress website" or "business website"
- Contact 5-10 developers with good ratings (4.5+ stars, 50+ reviews)
- Ask each one: "What's your price for a 5-page business website?" and "What's your turnaround time?"
- Bookmark your top 3 developers — these are your go-to team

**Day 5-7: Build Your Portfolio**
- Here's the secret: you don't need past clients to have a portfolio
- Ask your top Fiverr developer to show you their best 5-10 websites
- Take screenshots — these are your demo examples
- Alternatively, pay one developer $100-$150 to build a sample website for a fake business (this is your "investment")
- Create a simple PDF or folder with 5-8 website screenshots labeled by industry

### Week 2: First Leads (Days 8-14)

**Day 8-9: Buy Your First Batch of Leads**
- Go to Fiverr and search for "business leads" or "small business contacts"
- Buy 50-100 leads of local businesses (more details in Chapter 4)
- Cost: $25-$75

**Day 10-14: Make First Contact**
- Add all leads to your Excel spreadsheet
- Start reaching out: email, phone call, or message — whichever feels most natural
- Send 10-15 outreach messages per day
- Use the scripts in the Bonus Materials chapter
- Track every interaction in your spreadsheet

### Week 3: Conversations (Days 15-21)

**Day 15-21: Follow Up and Engage**
- Follow up with everyone who didn't respond (most people need 2-3 touches)
- Have real conversations with people who respond
- Ask questions: "Tell me about your business. Do you have a website? What's been holding you back?"
- Present your portfolio to anyone who shows interest
- Send at least 2 proposals this week

### Week 4: Close and Deliver (Days 22-30)

**Day 22-25: Close Your First Deal**
- Follow up on proposals
- Handle objections (Chapter 7 has your cheat sheet)
- Close at least 1 deal — even if it's at a lower price point ($1,000-$1,500) to build confidence
- Collect the deposit

**Day 26-30: Deliver Your First Website**
- Brief your Fiverr developer on the project
- Provide all client materials (logo, photos, text content)
- Review the first draft in 3-4 days
- Request revisions if needed
- Deliver to client, collect remaining balance

## The Compound Effect: Months 2, 3, and Beyond

Here's what most people don't realize: this business gets easier and more profitable every month. Here's why:

**Month 1:** You're learning. You're clumsy on the phone. Your spreadsheet is messy. You close 1-2 deals.

**Month 2:** You've refined your pitch. Your spreadsheet is dialed in. You have 1-2 happy clients as references. You close 3-5 deals. You also start offering monthly maintenance to Month 1 clients.

**Month 3:** You've got momentum. Referrals start coming in. Your confidence is through the roof. You close 5-8 deals. Recurring revenue starts building.

**Month 6:** You have 15-25 clients. 10 of them are on monthly maintenance at $149/month. That's $1,490/month in passive income PLUS you're still closing 4-6 new deals per month.

**Month 12:** You have 50+ clients. 30 are on monthly maintenance ($4,470/month recurring). You're closing 6-10 deals per month. You've hired a virtual assistant. Your annual income is over $100,000.

The machine compounds. Every client becomes a potential referral source. Every delivered website becomes a portfolio piece. Every month of maintenance adds to your recurring base.

This is not a sprint. It's a snowball rolling downhill. And it starts with 50 leads and an Excel spreadsheet.

That person could be you. After reading this book, it will be.

That's it. That's the whole business.

## The $200 Billion Web Industry You're Not Tapping Into

There are over 30 million small businesses in the United States alone. Globally, that number explodes into the hundreds of millions. Here's the shocking part: **nearly half of small businesses still don't have a website.** And the ones that do? Most of them have outdated, ugly, non-mobile-friendly sites from 2014.

The web design and development industry generates over $200 billion annually. And it's not dominated by big tech companies — it's driven by freelancers, small agencies, and yes, middlemen who connect businesses with developers.

Every single day, business owners are typing into Google:
- "I need a website for my restaurant"
- "How much does a website cost?"
- "Web designer near me"

These people have money. They have a problem. And they're willing to pay handsomely for someone to solve it. They don't care if you built the website yourself or if a team in the Philippines built it. They care about one thing: **does it look good and does it work?**

That's the gap you're going to fill.
